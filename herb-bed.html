Herb Bed — Layout & Varieties

L-shaped bed: 8×2 ft long arm + 4×2 ft short arm sharing a 2×2 ft corner.
Area = 16 + 8 − 4 = 20 sq ft. 12 herbs, 22 plants.

Layout & counts

HerbCountSpacing / notes
Corner (shared elbow, 2×2 ft)
Rosemary1Not reliably hardy in 5b — annual or pot up before frost
Marjoram2~8–10 in apart; tender, treat as annual
Short side, right of corner (2×2 ft)
Chives1 clumpHardy perennial; divide after 1–2 seasons
Anise Hyssop1Hardy perennial; can reach 2–3 ft, may outgrow 1 sq ft cell
Mint1Sunken bottomless pot — must be contained
Tarragon1French; hardy perennial, spreads by rhizome, divide every couple years
Long side, below corner (2×6 ft)
Sage1~18–24 in spread; woody, hardy
Thyme3~8 in apart; low edger, hardy
Oregano2~12 in apart; spreading, hardy
Chervil4~8 in apart; cool-season, bolts in heat
Dill3~10 in apart; bolts in heat, succession sow
Borage2~12–18 in apart; sprawls, self-seeds
